# Support Visit: Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

**English** · Hebrew version below (עברית בהמשך)

## 1. Who we are

Support Visit ("the app", "the service") is provided by [Vendor legal name], [company number], [address]
("we", "us"). Contact for privacy questions: [privacy email].

The service is used by businesses ("customers") to record field-service visits. Each customer decides
which information about its own clients is entered. For that information the customer is the database
owner (controller), and we process it on the customer's behalf. For the account information of the
people who use the app, we are the controller.

## 2. What we collect

**People who use the app (technicians, managers, admins)**
- Email address, used to sign in with a one-time code and to send reports and invitations.
- Role in the company (admin, manager, technician) and company membership.
- Sign-in codes and session tokens. We store these only as one-way cryptographic hashes.

**Information the customer enters about its own clients and work**
- Client details: name, contact person, email, phone, address, hourly rate, notes.
- Visit records: date, start and end times, visit type, work description, parts used, billing amounts.
- The client's signature and, optionally, the technician's signature.
- Photos taken or attached during a visit.
- The PDF reports and monthly reports generated from the above.
- Tasks (to-do items) and company profile: name, logo, address, company/VAT number, email template.

**Technical information (can be turned off in Settings → Privacy)**
- Crash reports: app version, Android version, phone model, and the technical stack trace (exception
  types and code lines only, with no messages and no content entered in the app).
- Anonymous usage counters: daily counts of events such as "visit submitted" per company, with no user
  identity and no content.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any data for advertising.

## 3. Why we use it (purposes and legal basis)

- To provide the service: sign-in, syncing visits between a company's phones, generating and emailing
  reports, team management (performance of our contract with the customer).
- To keep the service secure and prevent abuse, e.g. sending limits (legitimate interest).
- To fix crashes and understand which features are used (legitimate interest; you can opt out).
- To meet legal obligations.

## 4. Where it is stored

- **On the phone:** in the app's private storage, removed when the app is uninstalled or when you use
  *Settings → Clear data on this phone* or *Delete account*. It is not included in Google backups or
  phone-to-phone transfers.
- **On our server:** [hosting provider], [country/region]. Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Files are
  reachable only through short-lived signed links. Data of each company is kept separate from other
  companies.
- **Backups:** encrypted (AES-256) nightly backups, kept for 30 days.

## 5. Who we share it with

- **Email recipients the user chooses:** visit and monthly reports are emailed from our server to the
  addresses entered in the app (e.g. the client), through our email provider [SMTP provider].
- **Invoicing providers** (only if a customer turns this on): when a user creates an invoice, the
  monthly report's client and billing details are sent to the provider the customer connected (e.g.
  Green Invoice / Morning). Their privacy policy applies to that data.
- **Service providers** who host our server and send our email, under confidentiality obligations.
- **Authorities**, when required by law.

We do not sell personal data.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Visit, client and task data: as long as the customer's account is active, or until deleted in the
  app.
- **Deleting an account** (Settings → Delete account): the account is scheduled for deletion and can
  be restored by signing in again within 14 days. After that its visit reports, PDFs and photos are
  removed from the server and from the company's other phones, unless the user chose to keep them in
  the company's records. Copies in backups expire within 30 days after that.
- Crash reports: the most recent 5,000 reports across all customers. Usage counters: [period].
- Server logs: [period].

## 7. Your rights

Under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (including Amendment 13) and, where it applies,
the EU GDPR, you may ask to see, correct or delete personal data about you, and object to certain uses.
If your details were entered by a business that uses Support Visit (for example, you are one of its
clients), please contact that business first; we will help them respond. Otherwise contact us at
[privacy email]. You may also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority or, in the EU, to
your local data protection authority.

## 8. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS only), hashed sign-in codes and tokens, per-company separation, roles that
limit who sees what, an optional app lock (fingerprint, face or screen lock), encrypted backups, and
limits against abuse. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify customers and authorities about
serious incidents as the law requires.

## 9. Children

The service is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## 10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. For significant changes we will notify customers
by email or in the app.
